## Notes for sending (do not paste these)

- **The email must match their Play account.** Closed testing is granted per
  Google account. If someone signs in to Play with a different address from the
  one you mailed, the link shows "not available" and they will assume the app
  is broken rather than that they are on the wrong account. It is the single
  most common reason a tester silently drops out, which is why it has its own
  heading above rather than a line in a list.

- **Add every address in the Console first.** Testing → Closed testing →
  Testers, as an email list or a Google Group. The opt-in link does nothing for
  an address that is not on the list.

- **Check how many testers you need.** Personal Play developer accounts created
  from late 2023 onward must run a closed test with **at least 12 testers who
  stay opted in for 14 continuous days** before production access is granted.
  Recruit comfortably more than 12 — some will not complete the opt-in, and the
  count is of testers still enrolled, not of testers ever invited.

- **Emoji here, none in the listing.** Deliberate, and not a contradiction. A
  personal email is a medium where warmth reads as warmth. The store listing is
  read by a screen reader, indexed for search, and governed by a metadata
  policy that bars emoji from the title outright — see `README.md`.
