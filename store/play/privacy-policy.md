# Privacy Policy — Wonders and Hope

**Last updated: 2 August 2026**

Wonders and Hope is a Bible reading app published by Raimon Baudoin.

## The short version

The app collects nothing. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no
advertising, and no tracking of any kind. Nothing you do in the app is sent
anywhere, because there is nowhere for it to be sent — the developer operates no
server for this app.

## What stays on your device

Everything the app remembers is stored only on your device, in the app's own
private storage:

- **Verses you keep** — the reference, a short excerpt, the colour you chose,
  and any note you wrote.
- **Where you were reading** — the last wonder and the last chapter you opened,
  and your place in the guided tour.
- **Your settings** — reading size, colour theme, and the voice, speed and pitch
  used for reading aloud.

None of this is transmitted to the developer or shared with anyone. If you use
the export feature to save or send your kept verses, that file goes wherever
*you* choose to send it; the app has no copy of it and no knowledge of where it
went.

**Android backup.** Like most Android apps, this one participates in Android's
built-in Auto Backup. If you have device backup switched on, Android may copy
the app's private storage — including your kept verses and notes — to your own
Google account, so that your marks return when you set up a new phone. That
backup belongs to you and is governed by
[Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy); the developer
has no access to it and receives no copy. You can turn it off for this app in
Android's backup settings, and uninstalling removes the copy on the device
itself.

## What the app does over the internet

The app is designed to work offline. All 66 books of the World English Bible and
all 178 wonder cards ship inside the app, so reading, searching and listening
need no connection.

Two things can reach the internet, and only these:

1. **Typefaces.** The app uses the Inter, Merriweather and Playfair Display
   fonts, which are fetched from Google Fonts the first time they are needed and
   then cached on your device. This is a request for a font file. It is subject
   to [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). If you are
   offline the app renders in a system typeface instead and everything else
   works normally.
2. **Links you tap.** The links to the developer's own pages, on the More
   screen, open in your browser or in the relevant app. Nothing is sent unless
   you tap one.

## Reading aloud

Reading aloud uses the text-to-speech engine already installed on your device.
The text is handed to that engine locally. Some devices are configured to use a
network-based voice — whether that happens, and what it implies, is governed by
your device's own speech settings and the privacy policy of that engine's
provider (for example, Google or Samsung). The app does not choose this for you
and receives nothing back but audio.

## Permissions

- **Notifications** — used only to show the playback controls while a passage is
  being read aloud, so you can pause it from the lock screen. Declining costs
  you those controls and nothing else.
- **Foreground service (media playback)** — keeps the reading going when the app
  is not on screen.

The app requests no access to your contacts, camera, microphone, location,
files, or any other personal data.

## Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children. There is nothing to
delete, because nothing is gathered.
